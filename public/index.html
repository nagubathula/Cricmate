How to play

Bowling: pick a delivery type and a line, then hit Bowl. Mix it up — slower balls fool early swingers, yorkers are hard to attack, bouncers punish drives.

Batting: watch the ball and press a shot key as it reaches the glowing zone at your crease. The key you press is both your shot and your timing.

←/APull — leg side; great vs leg-line & bouncers
↑/WDrive — straight / off; great vs full balls
→/DCut — off side; for balls outside off
SpaceLoft — sixes or caught! Best vs spin & slower balls
↓/SBlock — safe, few runs

Don't press anything to leave the ball — but leave a straight one and you may lose your stumps.

Bowling keys: 1–6 delivery, Z/X/C line (leg/middle/off), Enter bowl.
